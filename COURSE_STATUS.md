## Editing principles

1. Keep all published teaching content in English, using British conventions and clear explanations for adults.
2. Preserve the textbook's lesson numbers and topic sequence.
3. Add examples and exceptions carefully, checking grammatical accuracy and whether multiple answers are acceptable.
4. Provide at least ten original/model examples, generous contextual practice, reliable answer keys and accessible mobile layouts.
5. Clearly distinguish manually extended lessons from study-guide editions.
6. Do not claim official Cambridge/Oxford accreditation, certification or guaranteed fluency.
7. Test navigation, accessibility, exercise marking and GitHub Pages publication after edits.
