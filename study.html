04 · Learn Step by Step

A simple method for deeper learning

1
Understand

Explain the main idea in simple English and find an example that illustrates it.

2
Notice

Underline the important words, endings or sentence structures in each example.

3
Change

Choose a model sentence and replace a name, place or idea while keeping the grammar correct.

4
Use

Imagine a real situation at work, in education or in daily life. Practise a suitable sentence.

5
Recall and check

Close your notes, say a sentence from memory and compare it with the model.

Daily practice: Spend at least 10–15 minutes on this lesson. Review difficult examples tomorrow, after a few days and again next week.
